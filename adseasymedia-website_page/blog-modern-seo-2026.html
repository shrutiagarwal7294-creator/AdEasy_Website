Modern SEO in 2026: Winning AI Overviews, GEO and AEO

AI Search✍️ AdsEasy Media Growth Team🗓 2026-06-18📖 8 min read

Search has evolved from traditional blue links to AI-generated answers, AI Overviews, conversational search and zero-click experiences. The uncomfortable implication: a strategy that only targets rankings now competes for a shrinking share of attention.

The three layers of modern visibility

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) wins the answer surfaces you can see — featured snippets, FAQ results, voice answers. It rewards direct-answer content, FAQ structure and clean structured data.

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) wins the surfaces you can't see directly — the citations AI engines choose when synthesizing answers. Entities, knowledge-graph presence and LLM-friendly content structure decide whether Gemini, Perplexity or ChatGPT Search quotes you or a competitor.

SXO (Search Experience Optimization) makes the click worth it: Core Web Vitals, journey mapping and conversion optimization turn visibility into revenue.

What to do this quarter

First, audit AI visibility: ask the major engines your customers' top 20 questions and record who gets cited. Second, restructure your highest-value pages into direct-answer formats with FAQ layers and schema. Third, build entity signals — consistent organization data, author profiles and citations from industry publications. Fourth, measure AI mentions and citations monthly alongside rankings.

Why E-E-A-T still decides winners

Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness and Trustworthiness remain critical ranking factors — and AI engines lean on the same signals when choosing sources. For YMYL niches like healthcare, law and finance, E-E-A-T optimization isn't optional; it's the entry fee.

The market context makes the case for acting early: the global SEO market is projected to grow from $74.9 billion in 2025 to $127.3 billion by 2030, with on-page SEO alone accounting for 42.3% of services. The winners of that expansion are being decided now — on AI surfaces most brands aren't watching yet.

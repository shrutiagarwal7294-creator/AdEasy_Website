Core Web Vitals in 2026: The Technical Checklist Before You Launch

Web Development✍️ AdsEasy Media Growth Team🗓 2026-07-20📖 6 min read

Speed isn't a nice-to-have anymore — it's a ranking factor, an ad Quality Score input, and the single biggest predictor of whether a visitor sticks around. Here's the checklist we run before any site goes live.

Largest Contentful Paint under 2.5 seconds

Compress and lazy-load images, preload critical fonts, and cut render-blocking scripts. Most sites lose their largest chunk of LCP time to unoptimized hero images alone.

Zero unexpected layout shift

Reserve space for images, ads and embeds before they load. Nothing erodes trust faster than a button jumping out from under a user's thumb mid-tap.

Fast, consistent response to every interaction

Defer non-critical JavaScript, minimize main-thread work, and test on a mid-range phone on real mobile network conditions — not just a developer's fibre connection and flagship device.

None of this is exotic engineering. It's discipline, applied consistently, checked before launch instead of discovered after rankings drop.

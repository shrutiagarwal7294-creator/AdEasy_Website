Our Engagement Models

Maximum Flexibility, Your Pace

Move your mobile project on at your convenient pace and benefit from a smoothly organized project flow.

💵

Fixed Cost

  • Fixed budget and timeline, agreed upfront
  • Transparency and predictability
  • Precise end-goal
  • Requires less supervision and management from the client
  • Ideal for short to medium-term projects and MVPs with fixed requirements
⏳

T&M (Time & Material)

  • Developers billed by time invested, not a fixed scope
  • Highly flexible
  • Effectively utilizes the agile methodology
  • Higher degree of project control
  • Ideal for projects with dynamic, undefined or complex scope requirements
👥

Dedicated Resources

  • A dedicated team of engineers, QA and project managers focused solely on your project
  • Transparent pricing with monthly billing
  • Tighter security standards
  • Predictable budget
  • Ideal for MVPs, startups, and product/software companies

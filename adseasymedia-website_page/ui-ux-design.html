E-commerce platform: checkout redesign lifted completion 33%

+33%Checkout completion
-28%Cart abandonment
4Steps removed

Challenge: An e-commerce platform's 7-step checkout confused first-time buyers and abandoned carts kept climbing.

Solution: Simplified to a 3-step checkout with inline validation, guest checkout, and clear progress indicators.

Execution: 5-week redesign, tested against the live flow with a phased rollout to validate impact before full launch.

“We removed friction we didn't even know we had until we watched users struggle with it.” — Head of Product, E-commerce platform

Healthcare portal: accessibility-first redesign widened reach

WCAG AACompliance achieved
+38%Portal task success
-52%Assisted-navigation calls

Challenge: A healthcare patient portal failed basic accessibility standards, excluding patients who relied on screen readers or keyboard navigation.

Solution: An accessibility-first redesign to WCAG AA standards, validated with real assistive-technology users.

Execution: 10-week redesign including a full accessibility audit, remediation and testing with users with disabilities.

“Patients who'd been calling for help for years could finally use the portal themselves.” — Digital Lead, Healthcare provider

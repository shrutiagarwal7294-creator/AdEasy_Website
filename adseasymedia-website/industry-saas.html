MetricFormulaWorked example
CAC — Customer Acquisition Cost(Cost of Sales + Cost of Marketing) ÷ New Customers AcquiredSpent $20,000 on marketing and $8,000 on sales to acquire 80 customers → ($20,000 + $8,000) ÷ 80 = $350
MRR — Monthly Recurring RevenueTotal Subscribers × Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)1,000 subscribers each paying $50/month → 1,000 × $50 = $50,000
CRC — Customer Retention CostTotal Retention Costs ÷ Retained CustomersSpent $5,000 on retention and retained 100 customers → $5,000 ÷ 100 = $50 per customer
